🔬 Research Project Experience

Project Work, AI Agent Functionality Testing

May 2026
Aalto University, Department of Information and Service Management | Supervisor: Prof. Liu Yong | Online
  • Conducted an independent functionality test of AI agents, examining how prompt-based control can shape agent behavior in collaborative human–AI interaction.
  • Designed two contrasting AI-agent profiles, high-mechanical intelligence and high-social intelligence, to test whether agents could maintain differentiated interaction styles across multi-turn conversations.
  • Evaluated same-topic responses in terms of style consistency, instruction following, controllability, and adaptive response behavior.
  • Found that AI agents can demonstrate clear behavioral differentiation and reliable output control when prompts are structured with explicit, testable rules, highlighting the role of user-defined control mechanisms in adaptive AI systems.
