Welcome!

I am a master’s graduate in Business and Economics / Governance of Digitalization from Åbo Akademi University. My background combines information systems, usability research, data analysis, and language teaching.

My recent work examines mobile language-learning usability and prompt-based control of AI agents. My proposed doctoral research focuses on how users delegate judgment to AI agents through dynamic interaction in mobile shopping.

Latest: My paper on pedagogically effective use has been accepted for presentation and publication in the ACIS 2026 proceedings.

💡 Research Direction

01Anthropomorphic social cues

What does existing research explain about anthropomorphic cues and judgment delegation, and where are the gaps?

Planned: systematic literature review
02Dynamic judgment delegation

How do users interpret agents’ cues and behaviour, and form or revise judgment delegation through ongoing interaction?

Planned: interviews · thematic analysis
03Mobile shopping scenarios

Which combinations of anthropomorphic cues and shopping-scenario characteristics are associated with delegated judgment?

Planned: survey · fsQCA
