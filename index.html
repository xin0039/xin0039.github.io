Welcome!

I am a master's graduate in Business and Economics, Governance of Digitalisation from Åbo Akademi University. My academic interests lie at the intersection of information systems, human–AI interaction, digital services, and data analysis.

My recent work focuses on usability evaluation of mobile language-learning services and prompt-based AI agent functionality testing. I am particularly interested in how users interact with intelligent digital services, and how AI systems can support learning, decision-making, and human–AI collaboration.

You can find my academic CV, research experience, publications, and selected academic activities on this page.

Aalto University, Department of Information and Service Management | Online | May 2026
  • Questions: Tested whether ChatGPT agents could be shaped into high-mechanical and high-social intelligence interaction styles, maintain these styles across conversations, and follow prompt-based output-control rules.
  • Methods: Conducted an independent online functionality test in a clean ChatGPT environment; designed and refined structured prompts; compared responses across same-topic multi-turn conversations.
